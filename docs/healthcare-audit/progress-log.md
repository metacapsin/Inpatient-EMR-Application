### 19. Patient registration (BUG-06, + USCDI demographics/NOK-guardian/unknown-patient/registration-time-consent) ✅

Three list-filter bugs fixed: `'19-35'` age range no longer silently resets to "all" (was missing from the frontend's allowed-values list); `recentlyCreated=today`/`thismonth` now actually filter server-side (previously only `last5recent` had any effect, the other two were accepted and silently ignored); the Patient column is sortable again and genuinely sorts by name (`sortField=fullName`), not creation date (it had no working sort control at all, and the one mapping that existed redirected name-sort to `createdOn`).
